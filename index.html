About the event

Indian Fight League (IFL) is India's first franchise-based professional MMA league, with city teams competing across a multi-event season. This card, Hyderabad vs North East, is a 9-bout showdown at Gachibowli Indoor Stadium, live on the evening of August 8, 2026. The main card broadcasts globally on UFC Fight Pass, with preliminary bouts streamed on YouTube.
